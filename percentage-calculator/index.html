How to Use

Step 1: Enter the percentage (e.g. 15 for 15% tip or discount rate).

Step 2: Enter the amount (e.g. 85 for an $85 bill or price).

All results update automatically — tip value, total with tax/tip, discounted price, and amount saved.

FAQ

How is the tip amount calculated?

Multiply the amount by the percentage divided by 100. Formula: Amount × (Percentage ÷ 100). Example: $85 × (15 ÷ 100) = $12.75.

How do I find the total including tax or tip?

Add the tip/percentage value to the original amount. Formula: Amount + (Amount × Percentage ÷ 100). Example: $85 + $12.75 = $97.75.

What's the discounted price?

Subtract the tip/percentage value from the original amount. Formula: Amount − (Amount × Percentage ÷ 100). Example: $85 − $12.75 = $72.25.

Can I use decimals?

Yes — enter any decimal percentage (e.g. 8.5 for tax rate) and any decimal amount. Results update in real time.
