How to Use the Percentage Calculator

Step 1: Choose a mode — Percentage for general percent calculations, or Discount for shopping savings.

Step 2: For "What is X% of Y?": enter the percentage and the total to find the portion. Example: 15% of $200 = $30.

Step 3: For "X is what % of Y?": enter the part and the whole. Example: 85 out of 100 = 85% — perfect for test scores.

Step 4: For "Percentage Change": enter original and new values to find the increase or decrease. Use this for price changes and investment returns.

Step 5: For the Discount Calculator: enter the original price and discount percentage to see your savings and final price instantly.

FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100. For example, 15% of 200 = 200 × (15 ÷ 100) = 30. Our calculator does this instantly — just enter your values above.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100. Formula: ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100. A positive result means an increase, negative means a decrease.

How does the discount calculator work?

It calculates the saved amount (Original × Discount% ÷ 100) and subtracts it from the original price. For example, $100 with 20% off saves you $20, so your final price is $80.

Can I use negative numbers?

Yes! For percentage change, entering a lower new value than the original will show a negative percentage change (decrease). For the discount calculator, negative discounts are treated as price increases.
