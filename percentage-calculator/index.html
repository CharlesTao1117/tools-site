❓ FAQ

How do I calculate 20% of a number?

Multiply the number by 0.20. For example, 20% of $80 = $80 × 0.20 = $16. Use Tab 1 above for quick results.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100. Example: (150-100)/100 × 100 = 50% increase. Use Tab 3 above.

What does "percent" mean?

"Percent" means "per hundred" or "out of 100." So 25% means 25 out of every 100, or 0.25 as a decimal.
