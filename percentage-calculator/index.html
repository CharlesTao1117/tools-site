FAQ

117|
118|
How is the percentage value calculated?
119|

Multiply the amount by the percentage, then divide by 100. For example, 15% of $85 = 85 × 15 ÷ 100 = $12.75.

120|
121|
122|
What's the difference between "With Tip/Tax" and "After Discount"?
123|

With Tip/Tax adds the percentage to the original (e.g., bill + tip = total you pay). After Discount subtracts the percentage (e.g., original price − discount = sale price).

What's a standard tip percentage?
127|

15% for good service, 18% for great service, 20% for excellent service. For large parties, 10–15% is common. Type any of these into the calculator above.
