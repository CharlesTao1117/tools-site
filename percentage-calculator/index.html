FAQ

How do I calculate 20% of a number?

Multiply the number by 0.20. For example, 20% of 80 = 80 × 0.20 = 16. Use the % of a Number tab for instant results.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original from the new, divide by the original, multiply by 100. Example: (150 − 100) ÷ 100 × 100 = 50% increase. Use the Percent Change tab.

What's a good tip percentage?

Standard tips: 15% for good service, 18–20% for great service, 10% for large parties or casual dining. Use the % of a Number tab to calculate instantly.
