How to Use the Lottery Draw

Step 1: Paste your list of participant names into the text area. You can use one name per line or separate names with commas — the tool auto-detects the format.

Step 2: Click 🎯 Draw Winner. Names will cycle quickly in a slot-machine style animation for about 1.5 seconds before landing on a random winner.

Step 3: The winner is displayed in a celebratory card with confetti animation. They are automatically removed from the pool so they won't be picked again.

Step 4: Keep drawing until all winners are selected, or click 🔄 Reset Draw to start over with all participants. Use 📋 Copy Results to export your draw history.

Your data is saved to your browser automatically — close the page and come back, everything is still there.

FAQ

How does the Lottery Draw work?

Paste or type a list of participant names (one per line or comma-separated), then click Draw Winner. The tool shuffles all remaining names using the Fisher-Yates algorithm and runs a quick cycling animation before landing on a random winner. Each winner is removed from the pool so they won't be drawn again unless you reset.

Is the draw truly random?

Yes. The Lottery Draw uses the Fisher-Yates shuffle algorithm — a proven, unbiased method for random permutation — combined with JavaScript's Math.random(). Every remaining participant has an equal chance of being selected on each draw. No weighting, no bias.

Can I draw multiple winners?

Absolutely! Click Draw Winner repeatedly to pick as many winners as you need. Each winner is removed from the remaining pool so there are never duplicates. You can see your full draw history with timestamps and use the Copy Results button to export everything.

Will my data be saved if I close the page?

Yes — your participant list and draw history are automatically saved to your browser's localStorage. When you return to the page, everything is restored exactly as you left it. Click Reset Draw to clear all saved data and start fresh.

What name formats can I paste?

You can paste names separated by newlines (one per line) or by commas. The tool auto-detects the separator and parses your list instantly — no manual formatting needed. Empty lines, extra spaces, and trailing commas are automatically cleaned up. Works with any language or character set.
