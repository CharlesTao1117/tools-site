FAQ

How much is 1 Philippine Peso to US Dollar?

The exchange rate fluctuates daily. Use this free real-time converter to get the current PHP to USD rate. As a rough reference, 1 PHP is approximately 0.017-0.018 USD.

How do you convert Philippine Peso to US Dollar?

To convert PHP to USD, divide the peso amount by the current exchange rate. For example if the rate is 56 PHP per 1 USD, then ₱1,000 ÷ 56 = $17.86 USD. This converter does the math instantly.

What currencies can I convert PHP to?

This converter supports PHP to USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, SGD, HKD, KRW, CNY, and more. Select your target currency from the dropdown menu.

Is the exchange rate live?

Yes, rates are fetched from a free exchange rate API updated daily on business days. Rates may differ slightly from bank or remittance center rates which include fees and margins.

How much is 1,000 Philippine Pesos in US Dollars?

At an approximate rate of 56 PHP per USD, 1,000 Philippine Pesos equals about 17.86 US Dollars. Enter the amount in the converter above for the current rate.

Why do banks and remittance centers give different rates?

The rate shown here is the mid-market (interbank) rate. Banks and remittance services like Palawan, Cebuana, Western Union, and PayPal add a margin (typically 1-5%) on top of the mid-market rate for their service fee.

How often are the exchange rates updated?

Rates are sourced from the European Central Bank and update once per business day. During weekends and holidays the rate shown is the last available rate from the previous business day.
