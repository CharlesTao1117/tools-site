❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Ikigai?
Ikigai (生き甲斐) is a Japanese life philosophy that means "a reason for being." Unlike a simple goal, ikigai is an ongoing sense of purpose that comes from the convergence of four areas: what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for.
Is this assessment accurate?
This tool is a self-reflection aid, not a clinical assessment. It helps you visualize where you currently stand across the four dimensions. Your true ikigai is discovered through real-world exploration, reflection, and practice over time.
What if all my scores are low?
That's completely normal, especially during transition periods. Start small: pick one dimension and invest 30 minutes a week exploring it. Try a new hobby (passion), volunteer (mission), take a course (vocation), or freelance a skill (profession). Small steps compound over time.
How often should I retake this?
Every 3–6 months is ideal. Life changes — new jobs, skills, relationships, and experiences shift your balance. Tracking the trend over time is more useful than any single score.
Is Ikigai different from happiness?
Yes. Happiness is a temporary emotion; ikigai is a sustained sense of direction and meaning. You can experience joy without ikigai, and you can have ikigai even during difficult times because it gives you a reason to keep going.
