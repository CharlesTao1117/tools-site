Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Free Online Tools ("we", "us", or "our") handles data when you use our website at anycalculator.site (the "Site"). We are committed to being transparent about what data we do and do not collect, and to giving you control over your information.

1. Data Collection by Our Tools

All tools on this Site are pure frontend applications. This means every calculation, generation, and operation happens entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. The data you enter into any tool — such as your weight for the BMI Calculator, text for the QR Code Generator, or birthdate for the Age Calculator — is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to, stored on, or readable by our servers.

Specifically:

  • BMI Calculator, Age Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Date Calculator, Add/Subtract Calculator: All inputs and results stay in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
  • QR Code Generator, Barcode Generator: The text or URL you enter is encoded into images locally. No server-side processing occurs.
  • Password Generator: Passwords are generated using your browser's cryptographic random functions and never leave your device.
  • Invoice Generator, Resume Generator: Document content is entered and rendered in your browser. Downloads are generated client-side.
  • Peso Converter: Exchange rates are fetched from public APIs at load time. Your conversion inputs are processed locally.
  • Nursing Calculators (APGAR, Creatinine Clearance, IV Drip Rate, etc.): All clinical inputs and calculations remain in the browser. No patient data is transmitted or stored.

2. Feedback Form

The feedback form on our homepage allows you to optionally submit your name, email address, and a message. When you submit this form, the data is sent via FormSubmit (a third-party form processing service) to our support inbox at cs@anycalculator.site. This is the only situation where data you provide leaves your browser, and it only happens when you explicitly choose to submit the form. We use this information solely to respond to your feedback and do not add you to any marketing list.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

This Site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.
  • For more information about how Google uses data when you use partner sites, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Our AdSense publisher ID is ca-pub-2318270958837564. We do not control which specific ads Google displays, nor do we have access to the individualized data Google may collect through its advertising services.

4. Google Analytics

This Site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as how many visitors use each tool, which browsers are most common, and which countries visitors come from. Google Analytics collects anonymized, aggregate data including page views, session duration, and approximate geographic location. It does not collect the data you enter into any tool.

You can learn more about how Google Analytics collects and processes data at Google's Cookie Technologies page. You may opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data Security

Because all tool processing happens locally in your browser, there is no server-side database of user inputs to protect or breach. The only data that reaches our infrastructure is the voluntary feedback form submissions (handled by FormSubmit) and aggregate analytics data (handled by Google). We rely on the security practices of these third-party providers for that data.

6. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through the feedback form, please contact us at cs@anycalculator.site and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data
  • Object to processing of your personal data

Since we do not collect tool input data, the only personal data we may hold is from voluntary feedback form submissions. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at cs@anycalculator.site.

8. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to external websites (e.g. GitHub, Google policy pages, npm CDN-hosted libraries). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information to them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at cs@anycalculator.site or visit our Contact page.
