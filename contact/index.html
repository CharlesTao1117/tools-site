Contact Us

We welcome bug reports, feature requests, feedback, and partnership inquiries. Here is how to reach us:

Email

cs@anycalculator.site

For bug reports, feature requests, and general questions about our tools.

Typical response: 2-3 business days

What to Include

To help us resolve your request quickly, please include:

  • The tool name and URL where the issue occurred
  • Steps to reproduce (for bug reports)
  • Your browser and device (e.g. Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone)
  • Screenshots if applicable

Feedback Form

You can also use the feedback form at the bottom of our homepage. It sends an email directly to our support inbox — no account needed.

Business Inquiries

For partnerships, sponsorships, or API access, please email cs@anycalculator.site with the subject line "Business Inquiry."

Response Time

We are a small team. We typically respond within 2-3 business days. For urgent issues affecting tool accuracy (e.g. incorrect calculation results), we prioritize fixes and may respond sooner.

What We Cannot Help With

Our tools are provided for general informational purposes only. We cannot provide medical advice, financial advice, or professional consultations. For such matters, please consult a qualified professional.
