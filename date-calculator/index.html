❓ FAQ

Does the date calculator count weekends?

This calculator counts all calendar days (including weekends and holidays). For business days only (Mon-Fri), you can manually exclude weekends from the result.

Does it account for leap years?

Yes! The JavaScript Date object automatically handles leap years (February 29) and varying month lengths correctly for all dates from year 1 to 9999.

What can I use this date calculator for?

Planning events, calculating project deadlines, counting days until a vacation or birthday, determining how many days since a historical event, pregnancy due dates, and more.
