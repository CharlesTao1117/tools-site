How to Use the Date Calculator

  1. Days Between mode: Pick a start date and end date. The calculator instantly shows the exact difference in days, weeks, months, years, and workdays (Monday–Friday).
  2. Add/Subtract mode: Choose a start date, toggle + or −, enter an amount, and select a unit (days, weeks, months, or years). The result date updates as you type.
  3. Use the results for project planning, deadline tracking, event countdowns, or calculating due dates.
  4. All calculations run locally in your browser — no data is ever sent anywhere.

FAQ

How are workdays calculated?

Workdays count only Monday through Friday, excluding Saturdays and Sundays. The calculation includes both the start and end dates if they fall on weekdays, giving you the total number of business days in the range.

How does month arithmetic handle different month lengths?

When adding or subtracting months, the calculator preserves the day-of-month where possible. For example, adding 1 month to January 31 yields February 28 (or 29 in leap years) — it clamps to the last valid day of the target month.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes — all calculations use JavaScript's built-in Date object, which correctly handles leap years, daylight saving transitions, and the Gregorian calendar.

Can I calculate dates in the past?

Absolutely. You can subtract to find a past date, or use the Days Between mode with past dates. The tool handles any date from year 1 to year 9999.
