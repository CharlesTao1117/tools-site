Frequently Asked Questions

Does it count weekends?

This counts all calendar days, including weekends and holidays. For business days only (excluding weekends), you would need to manually filter those from the result.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. The JavaScript Date object correctly handles February 29 and varying month lengths for all dates from year 1 to 9999, so leap years are fully accounted for.
