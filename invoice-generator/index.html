How to Use the Invoice Generator

Step 1: Fill in your business details (name, email, address) in the From section.

Step 2: Enter your client's information in the Bill To section.

Step 3: Adjust the invoice number, issue date, and due date as needed.

Step 4: Add line items with descriptions, quantities, and rates. Click Add Line Item to add more rows or the × button to remove them.

Step 5: Set the tax rate, discount (percentage or fixed amount), currency, and payment notes.

Step 6: Review the live preview on the right side, then click Print Invoice or Download PDF.

FAQ

How does invoice numbering work?

The invoice number is auto-generated as INV-0001 but you can edit it to any format you prefer. Common formats include INV-2024-001, or simply use sequential numbers. The tool remembers your last number only during the current session.

How is tax handled?

Enter your desired tax rate as a percentage (e.g., 8 for 8%). The tax is calculated on the subtotal (sum of all line items before discounts). If you don't need tax, set it to 0%.

What currencies are supported?

We support 8 currencies: USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), PHP (₱), TWD (NT$), CNY (¥), and KRW (₩). Select your preferred currency from the dropdown — the preview and printed invoice will use the corresponding symbol.

Can I set payment terms?

Yes! Use the Notes field at the bottom of the form to include payment terms, due date reminders, or any special instructions for your client. This text appears at the bottom of the invoice preview.

How do I print or save as PDF?

Click Print Invoice to open your browser's print dialog. Choose "Save as PDF" as the destination to create a PDF file. The print layout is optimized to show only the invoice (no UI elements) on a clean page.
