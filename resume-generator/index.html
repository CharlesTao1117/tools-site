How to Use

  1. Fill in your details — Enter your personal information, professional summary, work experience, education, and skills in the input panels on the left.
  2. Choose a template — Select from Classic (serif-style, traditional), Modern (clean sans-serif), or Minimal (sleek, lots of whitespace). The preview updates instantly.
  3. Add skills as tags — Type skills separated by commas and they auto-convert into pill badges. Click the ✕ on any tag to remove it.
  4. Add multiple entries — Use the + Add Experience and + Add Education buttons to add multiple work and education entries. Remove any entry with the ✕ button.
  5. Print or download — Click Print Resume to print directly, or Download PDF to open the print dialog where you can select Save as PDF as your destination.

FAQ

Is this resume ATS-compatible?

Yes! All three templates (Classic, Modern, Minimal) use a clean, single-column layout that's optimized for Applicant Tracking Systems. To maximize ATS compatibility, stick to standard section headings like "Work Experience" and "Education", avoid tables, and keep formatting simple.

What's the difference between the templates?

Classic: Uses Georgia serif font with traditional bordered sections — great for conservative industries like finance and law.
Modern: Clean Inter sans-serif with accent-colored job titles and subtle separators — perfect for tech and creative roles.
Minimal: Sleek no-frills layout with maximum whitespace and thin borders — ideal for design and startup positions.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. Click the Download PDF button to open your browser's print dialog, then select Save as PDF as the destination. Your resume will render in a clean print layout with no UI elements. Some browsers also allow you to save directly as PDF from the print dialog.

Is my data saved or shared?

Your privacy is important. All data stays entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to any server. The resume generator processes everything client-side using JavaScript. You can refresh the page and your data will be lost (it's not stored), so be sure to print or download your resume before leaving.

What file formats are supported?

The generator produces a live HTML preview that can be printed or saved as PDF. For other formats like DOCX or plain text, we recommend copying the content from the preview and pasting it into Word or Google Docs for further editing.
