How to Use

  1. Fill in your details — Enter your personal information, professional summary, work experience, education, and skills in the input panels on the left. Everything auto-saves to your browser.
  2. Browse templates — Scroll through 8 templates in the carousel above. Each card shows a miniature preview of its layout. Click to select — the live preview updates instantly.
  3. Customize appearance — After selecting a template, use the font size dropdown and accent color picker to fine-tune the look. Choose from preset colors or pick any custom color.
  4. Check ATS compatibility — Click the Check ATS button to analyze your resume for Applicant Tracking System readability. Get a score, detailed checks, and improvement suggestions.
  5. Add skills as tags — Type skills separated by commas and they auto-convert into pill badges. Click the ✕ on any tag to remove it.
  6. Add multiple entries — Use + Add Experience and + Add Education to add multiple entries. Remove any entry with the ✕ button.
  7. Print or download — Click Print Resume to print directly, or Download PDF to open the print dialog where you can select Save as PDF.

FAQ

Is this resume ATS-compatible?

Yes! All 8 templates use clean, single-column (or ATS-friendly) layouts optimized for Applicant Tracking Systems. Use the built-in Check ATS button to get a detailed compatibility score with specific improvement suggestions. Standard section headings, bullet points, and simple formatting yield the best results.

What templates are available?

There are 8 templates to choose from:
Classic 📜 — Georgia serif, bordered sections, traditional conservative style.
Modern ✨ — Inter sans-serif, accent-colored titles, clean and contemporary.
Minimal ◻ — No-frills, maximum whitespace, thin borders, sleek design.
Professional 💼 — Dark top bar with white text name, formal styling.
Creative 🎨 — Colored section headers with gradient name, playful layout.
Executive 👔 — Elegant serif with gold accents, premium conservative feel.
Compact 📋 — Dense layout with smaller fonts, optimized for 1-page resumes.
Split ↔️ — Two-column side layout with left sidebar and right content area.

Can I customize fonts and colors?

Yes! After selecting a template, use the font size dropdown (Small/Medium/Large) to adjust text sizing, and the accent color picker to change the theme color. You can choose from preset colors or pick any custom color using the color input.

Is my data saved if I refresh?

Yes! Your resume data is automatically saved to your browser's localStorage as you type. If you refresh the page or come back later, your work will be restored. Your data never leaves your device — it stays entirely in your browser for privacy.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. Click the Download PDF button to open your browser's print dialog, then select Save as PDF as the destination. Your resume will render in a clean print layout with no UI elements.

What file formats are supported?

The generator produces a live HTML preview that can be printed or saved as PDF. For other formats like DOCX or plain text, copy the content from the preview and paste it into Word or Google Docs.
