FAQ

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 0.20 to get the discount amount, then subtract from the original. $100 × 0.20 = $20 off → $80 final price.

Can I stack discounts?

This calculator handles single discounts. For stacked discounts, apply them sequentially — order matters.
