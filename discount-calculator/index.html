FAQ

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 0.20 to get the discount amount, then subtract from the original. For example: $100 × 0.20 = $20 off → $80 final price.

Can I stack discounts?

This calculator handles single discounts. For stacked discounts, apply them sequentially — the order matters and the total discount percentage will differ from simply adding them.

What's the difference between % Off and $ Off?

% Off applies a percentage-based discount (e.g., 20% off $100 = $80). $ Off subtracts a fixed amount (e.g., $15 off $100 = $85). Use % Off for percentage sales and $ Off for coupon codes with a fixed value.

How do I calculate the discount percentage from a sale price?

Divide the discount amount by the original price, then multiply by 100. For example: if an item is marked down from $50 to $40, the discount is ($50 − $40) ÷ $50 × 100 = 20% off.
