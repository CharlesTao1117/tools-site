❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of content can a QR Code store?

URLs, plain text, phone numbers, email addresses, SMS, WiFi settings, vCard contacts, and more. The most common use is encoding a URL so people can scan it to open a website.

Do I need an app to scan QR codes?

Modern smartphones (iOS 11+ and Android 9+) can scan QR codes directly with the built-in camera app. No additional app required.

Do QR codes expire?

No, QR codes themselves never expire. They are static images that encode the content directly. As long as the linked website or information still exists, the QR code will always work.

What format can I download?

Currently PNG format with transparent background. Suitable for print, web, and digital signage. SVG vector format is coming in a future update for higher resolution needs.

Is the resolution good enough for printing?

The "Large (400px)" size works for most print needs. For higher resolution, SVG vector export will be available in a future version.
