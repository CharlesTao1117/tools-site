How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your birth date using the date picker — the calculator instantly shows your exact age
  2. Optionally change the target date (defaults to today) to calculate your age at any point in the past or future
  3. View the detailed breakdown: years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds
  4. Check the totals section for total months, weeks, days, and hours since birth

FAQ

How is age calculated in years, months, and days?

The calculator computes the full calendar difference between your birth date and the target date. It first counts full years, then remaining months, then remaining days — accounting for varying month lengths and leap years.

Can I calculate age for a future date?

Yes — set the target date to any future date to see your age at that point, or calculate how old someone will be on a specific upcoming date.

Does this work for any two dates?

Absolutely. You can use it as a date difference calculator by entering any two dates — not just a birth date. Find the duration between any two dates in years, months, days, and total time units.

Is my data stored or shared?

No — all calculations happen in your browser using JavaScript. No dates are ever sent to any server or stored anywhere.
