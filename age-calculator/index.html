How to Use

  1. Enter the date of birth
  2. The "as of" date defaults to today
  3. Age updates instantly — years, months, days, total days lived, and a visual birthday progress ring

FAQ

How is age calculated?

Your browser compares the birth date to the chosen "as of" date, accounting for leap years (February 29) and varying month lengths. The result shows whole years, remaining months, and remaining days.

Can I use past or future dates?

Yes! Change the "as of" date to any date in the past or future to calculate age at that specific moment. Want to know how old you'll be on a future milestone? Just set the date.

What is the birthday progress ring?

The ring shows how far through your current year of life you are — from your last birthday to your next one. When it reaches 100%, it's time to celebrate!

Why does "Total Days Lived" matter?

It's a tangible perspective on your age — seeing the exact number of days you've been alive can be a powerful reminder of time. The count includes leap days.
