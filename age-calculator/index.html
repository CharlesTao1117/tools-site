How to Use

  1. Enter the date of birth

FAQ

How is age calculated?

Your browser compares the birth date to today's date, accounting for leap years and varying month lengths.

Can I use past dates?

Yes! Change the "as of" date to any date in the past or future to calculate age at that specific time.
