📖 How to Use

  1. Enter your date of birth (or any starting date)

  3. Click "Calculate Age" to see your exact age
  4. View your age in years, months, days, and total days since birth

❓ FAQ

How is my exact age calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your birth date to today's date. The calculator accounts for leap years and varying month lengths to give you the most precise result possible.

Why does age matter for health?

Age is a key factor in medical screening recommendations, medication dosing, BMI interpretation for children, and many health guidelines. Always provide your accurate age when consulting healthcare providers.

Does this calculator work for past dates?

Yes! Change the "As of Date" to any date in the past or future to calculate age at that specific point in time. This is useful for historical research or planning future milestones.
