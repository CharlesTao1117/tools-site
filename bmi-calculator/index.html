📖 What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is an internationally used measure for body weight assessment. The formula is:

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

BMI is the most widely used weight assessment tool. While it doesn't directly measure body fat, it correlates highly with body fat percentage for most adults and serves as a useful initial health screening indicator.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BMI formula?

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²). For example: height 170 cm (1.7 m), weight 65 kg → BMI = 65 ÷ (1.7 × 1.7) = 22.5, which is in the normal range.

What is a normal BMI?

For Asian adults, the normal BMI range is 18.5 to 24.0. Below 18.5 is underweight, 24.0 to 27.0 is overweight, and above 27.0 is obese.

Is BMI accurate? What are its limitations?

BMI is a convenient screening tool but has limitations: (1) It cannot distinguish muscle from fat, so athletes may be miscategorized as overweight. (2) Not suitable for pregnant women, the elderly, or bodybuilders. (3) Doesn't reflect fat distribution. Combine with waist measurement and body fat percentage for a fuller picture.

What should I do if my BMI is high?

Consult a doctor or nutritionist for a personalized diet and exercise plan. General principles: control calorie intake, eat more vegetables, reduce processed foods, and aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise per week. Avoid extreme dieting.

Can children and teenagers use BMI?

BMI interpretation for children and teens differs from adults — it must be compared to age and sex-specific growth percentile charts. This calculator is for adults 18+. For children, please consult a pediatrician or growth chart.
