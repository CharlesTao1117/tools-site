How to Use the BMI Calculator

Step 1: Select your preferred unit — Metric (cm/kg) or Imperial (feet/inches/lbs). Most users find Metric simpler.

Step 2: Enter your height and weight. The BMI result updates instantly — no button to click.

Step 3: Check your BMI category on the gauge: Underweight (blue), Normal (green), Overweight (yellow), Mild Obesity (orange), or Severe Obesity (red).

Step 4: Enter your age and select your sex to see your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) — the calories your body needs at rest.

Use your BMR as a baseline for meal planning. If your BMI is outside the normal range, consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.

FAQ

What is a healthy BMI range?

For Asian adults, 18.5–24.0 is the normal BMI range. Below 18.5 indicates underweight, 24.0–27.0 is overweight, 27.0–30.0 is mild obesity, and above 30.0 is severe obesity. These ranges follow Taiwan MOHW guidelines which are stricter than WHO standards.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated using the formula: weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared (BMI = kg/m²). For example, a person weighing 70 kg at 175 cm tall has a BMI of 22.9, which falls in the normal range.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a useful screening tool but has limitations. It doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat, so athletes may show as 'overweight' despite low body fat. It's also less accurate for pregnant women, the elderly, and children.

What is BMR and why does it matter?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the calories your body burns at complete rest. Knowing your BMR helps you plan your daily calorie intake for weight management. This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the most accurate BMR formula.

What should I do if my BMI is high?

Consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice. General recommendations include a balanced diet rich in vegetables, regular exercise (150+ minutes per week), adequate sleep, and stress management.

Can I use this calculator in imperial units?

Yes! Toggle between Metric (cm/kg) and Imperial (feet/inches/lbs) using the unit switch. The calculator automatically converts and shows results in both systems.

What's the difference between BMI for men and women?

BMI uses the same formula for both genders, but body composition differs. The calculator provides a gender-specific BMR estimate alongside your BMI for a more complete health picture.
