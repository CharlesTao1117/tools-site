What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a widely-used screening tool that estimates body fat based on height and weight. While it doesn't measure body fat directly, it correlates well for most adults.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

FAQ

What's a normal BMI?

For Asian adults, 18.5–24.0 is the normal range. Below 18.5 is underweight, 24.0+ is overweight, and 27.0+ is obese per Taiwan MOHW guidelines.

Is BMI accurate?

BMI is a useful screening tool but has limits: it can't distinguish muscle from fat, isn't suitable for athletes/pregnant women/elderly, and doesn't measure fat distribution.

What should I do if my BMI is high?

Consult a doctor or nutritionist. General advice: control calories, eat more vegetables, reduce processed foods, and aim for 150+ minutes of exercise per week.

What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body needs at complete rest. The Mifflin-St Jeor equation used here is the most accurate formula for estimating BMR based on weight, height, age, and sex.
