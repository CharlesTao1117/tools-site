04 — Questions

Before you submit.

Clear answers to the questions customers usually ask first.

Do I pay before receiving a quote?
No. Submit the document details first. We confirm the scope, price, and GCash instructions before payment.
What counts as one document?
One independent official document counts as one document. A PSA Birth Certificate and an NBI Clearance count as two documents even when uploaded together.
Can I submit an uncommon document?
Yes. Documents outside the standard list require manual review before we confirm the quote and delivery schedule.
Is acceptance by an authority guaranteed?
No. Requirements vary by destination, purpose, and receiving authority. Additional authentication or supporting documents may be required.
