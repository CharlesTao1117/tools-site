📖 How to Use This Countdown Tool

Taiwan's national exam calendar runs on an annual rhythm: the Preliminary exam opens the year in January, the Higher and Ordinary civil service exams anchor the summer, and the Local Government Special exam closes the year in December. This page turns that calendar into a live countdown so you always know exactly how much preparation time is left.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Check the featured timer: The large countdown at the top tracks the very next exam on the 2027 calendar, ticking down by the second — days, hours, minutes, and seconds.
  2. Filter by category: Use the filter buttons to show only civil service exams (government positions) or professional licenses (law, accounting, nursing, and other technical fields).
  3. Watch the registration deadline: Each card shows the planned registration window's final date. Registration windows are short — often just 7 to 10 days — so mark them in your calendar.
  4. Link to official details: The "Official details →" link on each card opens the Ministry of Examination (考選部) website, where you can verify the announcement and download the exam bulletin.
💡 Tip: Plan your study backward from the exam date, not forward from today. If your target exam is 200 days away, a realistic plan is 3 content passes plus 2 full mock-exam cycles — schedule them first, then fill in daily review.

🔬 Where the Dates Come From

Every date on this page is taken from the Ministry of Examination (考選部) annual exam calendar — the 考試期日計畫表 published for ROC Year 116 (2027). That calendar lists 20 numbered exam events with planned registration windows and planned exam dates.

How the Data Is Structured

  • Multi-stage exams are listed by stage. For example, the Lawyer exam runs as a 1st written stage (2027-08-07) and a separate 2nd computerized stage later in the year. This page tracks the 1st written stage, which is the milestone most candidates count down to.
  • Registration windows are short. Most national exams open registration for roughly 7–10 days. The calendar's final registration date is shown on each card.
  • Planned ≠ final. The ministry's calendar notes that all listed dates are provisional and subject to change until each exam's formal announcement (簡章) is published — typically about two months before the exam date.
💡 Tip: For the definitive dates, check the official exam announcement on the Ministry of Examination website once the bulletin for your target exam is published.

🎯 Study Planning Tips for the 2027 Calendar

The countdown is most useful when it drives a plan. Here's how different candidates use the 2027 calendar:

  • Stacking two exams: The Ordinary (July 2–3) and Higher (July 4–6) exams run back-to-back, and most subjects overlap — thousands of candidates register for both in the same cycle. If you're one of them, plan your heaviest review in May–June.
  • The January start: The Preliminary exam is the year's first milestone and requires no university degree. Its registration window (October 27 – November 5, 2026) closes months before the exam — the most commonly missed deadline on the calendar.
  • December finish: The Local Government exam closes the year. Candidates who miss the July exams often retarget December, which buys roughly 5 extra months of preparation.
  • License cycles: Nursing runs three sessions a year (February, late July–August window, and November), so a missed session is never more than a few months from the next attempt.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When is Taiwan's 2027 civil service exam?
The Higher Civil Service Exam Level 3 (高考三級) is planned for July 4–6, 2027, and the Ordinary Civil Service Exam (普考) for July 2–3, 2027. Registration is planned for March 9–18, 2027. Both dates come from the Ministry of Examination's 2027 calendar and remain provisional until the formal exam announcement.
When is the 2027 Preliminary Civil Service Exam (初等考試)?
January 9, 2027 (Saturday), held across 15 exam regions island-wide. Registration runs October 27 to November 5, 2026, and about 528 positions are tentatively planned across 16 categories. It's the entry-level national exam with no university degree requirement.
When is the 2027 Local Government Special Exam (地方特考)?
The 1st-stage written exam is planned for December 4–6, 2027, with registration from September 7–16, 2027. Results are typically announced the following March, and selected candidates are assigned to positions within the county or city they applied for.
Are these exam dates final?
No — they are planned dates from the Ministry of Examination's annual calendar. The ministry's calendar explicitly notes that all exam types, levels, and dates are provisional and may change; the binding dates are the ones in each exam's formal announcement, usually published about two months before the exam.
How far in advance should I start preparing?
For the July civil service exams, most successful candidates study 6–12 months. A practical benchmark: aim for three full passes through the syllabus and at least two timed mock exams. If your countdown shows under 90 days, switch from learning new material to drills and past-paper practice.
Is this countdown tool free?
Yes — completely free, no registration required. The page updates automatically, works on mobile and desktop, and you can bookmark it to check the remaining time any time. The dates shown are from the official Ministry of Examination calendar.
